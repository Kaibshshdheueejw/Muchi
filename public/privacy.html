Muchi Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Muchi collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Muchi website, progressive web app, or Muchi mobile application.

1. Who operates Muchi

Muchi is operated by Mochi. Questions about privacy can be sent to twiarimascord@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, Muchi may process the following information:

  • Google account information: your Google account identifier, email address, name, and profile image received during Google sign-in. Muchi does not receive or store your Google password.
  • YouTube information, only after you choose Connect YouTube: liked-video information, playlist information, and identifiers needed to perform a YouTube action that you request. Muchi does not request YouTube access as a requirement for ordinary Muchi sign-in.
  • Muchi library and playback information: local playlists, liked tracks, recent tracks, queue information, preferences, downloads, and playback settings. Much of this information is stored locally on your browser or device.
  • Search and music requests: searches, song identifiers, artist names, lyrics requests, radio requests, and related music requests needed to provide the requested feature.
  • Muchi Agent messages: if you use Muchi Agent, your messages and limited user-facing context such as the current track, queue, recent tracks, liked tracks, playlist names, and country may be processed to answer your request.
  • Technical information: basic request, browser, device, security, and diagnostic information that hosting and security providers normally process when delivering a web service.

3. How we use information

  • To create and maintain your Muchi account and session.
  • To provide playback, search, lyrics, radio, library, playlist, and discovery features.
  • To show your YouTube liked videos and playlists after you separately authorize YouTube.
  • To perform a YouTube rating or playlist action only after you request that action.
  • To answer Muchi Agent requests, identify songs, recommend music, and prepare user-confirmed playlists.
  • To protect the service, prevent abuse, diagnose errors, and maintain reliability.
  • To comply with applicable law and respond to valid legal requests.

4. Google and YouTube data

Muchi uses Google OAuth for sign-in and uses YouTube authorization as an optional, separate connection. Muchi's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Muchi uses YouTube data only to provide the user-facing features described in the app: displaying the user's own liked videos and playlists, rating a selected video, or adding a selected video to a selected playlist. Muchi does not sell Google or YouTube data, use it for advertising, or use it for unrelated purposes.

5. Service providers and sharing

Muchi may process information through service providers needed to operate the app, including:

  • Google and YouTube APIs for authentication and optional YouTube features.
  • Cloudflare Workers, D1, KV, and related infrastructure for hosting, sessions, and app data.
  • Music metadata, lyrics, radio, and streaming providers requested by the feature you use.
  • NVIDIA hosted inference when you use Muchi Agent. Agent messages and limited app context may be sent to NVIDIA to generate a response.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share information with third parties except as needed to provide requested features, operate and secure Muchi, comply with law, or protect users and the service.

6. Storage and retention

Web sessions use a secure server-side session cookie. Google and YouTube OAuth tokens are kept server-side and are not intentionally exposed as raw tokens to the Muchi interface. Local playlists, preferences, queue data, and some playback history may remain in your browser or device until you remove them or clear app storage.

We retain account and authorization information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested features, maintain security, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of account information by contacting us.

7. Your choices

  • You may use Muchi without connecting YouTube.
  • You may disconnect YouTube from Muchi settings or revoke Muchi from your Google Account security settings.
  • You may sign out of Muchi and clear local browser or device storage.
  • You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information by contacting twiarimascord@gmail.com.

8. Children

Muchi is not directed to children under 13, or the applicable minimum age in the country where the user lives. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through Muchi.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards for the information handled by Muchi. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Never enter passwords, financial information, private keys, or other highly sensitive information into Muchi Agent messages.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Muchi's features, providers, or legal obligations change. The updated policy will be posted at this URL with a new revision date.

11. Contact
